# Parking lot

Dated, unstructured ideas. Recorded so they are not lost. **Not scheduled, not
promised, and not commitments.**

This file exists so that a good suggestion has somewhere to go that is not
`docs/NON-GOALS.md` and not the roadmap. Rejecting an idea outright discourages
the person who had it; scheduling it creates an obligation the program cannot
meet. Writing it down here does neither.

## Rules

- **One entry per idea.** Date it, sign it if you like, and write a sentence or
  two. Do not write a design.
- **No status field, no owner, no priority.** Anything with those belongs in a
  trade or an issue. The absence of structure is the point: structure implies
  intent.
- **Nothing here is scheduled.** An item moves out of this file by becoming a
  trade, an ADR proposal, or a roadmap item, through the ordinary process. It
  does not graduate by sitting here long enough.
- **Deleting is fine.** An idea that has been here for years and still
  interests nobody can go. It is in the history.
- An item that conflicts with `docs/NON-GOALS.md` can still be recorded here.
  Recording is not adopting, and the non-goal still requires an ADR to move.

## Format

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD Short title

One or two sentences. What the idea is, and what made someone think of it.
```
