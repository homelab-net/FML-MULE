What a pass is allowed to meanOpen the repository

A clean exit is SIMULATED. The digital twin ran the node logic against fakes. The suite is still test/flatsat. CI has no radio, battery, or enclosure. A node that cannot serve users reports a fault rather than GREEN. Authentication is not in this suite.

Before buying anything: RF and compute that stay useful either way, one fit article, no battery until the pack decision, and the relay node because two nodes cannot show rerouting. Part numbers stay off this page. Naming a board would look like a decision.
